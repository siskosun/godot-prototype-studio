# Focused final review

Use the lens relevant to the requested decision. For a substantial prototype handoff, make one concise pass across all three lenses without pretending to employ three independent people. Findings must cite actual design/build evidence and distinguish observation from judgment.

**Game designer:** Does the completed session expose the promised verbs, decisions, feedback, and differentiating interaction? Are failures legible and recurring choices genuinely useful? Did reference comparison improve the design rather than create a feature mashup?

**Nontechnical creator:** Can the recipient run the delivered artifact, understand controls, recover or restart, and identify what is incomplete without interpreting logs? Were ordinary decisions and technical fixes handled rather than repeatedly pushed back to them?

**Publisher:** Can the slice communicate its player promise with a representative play path and coherent presentation? What is actually demonstrated versus untested: technical feasibility, control/readability, experience, production scalability, audience, or commercial fit? Avoid a mandatory business case for an internal mechanic probe.

Fix required defects within scope and rerun affected evidence. Defer speculative improvements. Review is an internal completion check, not an automatic human approval stage. Use the optional review packet only when someone needs a durable review artifact.
