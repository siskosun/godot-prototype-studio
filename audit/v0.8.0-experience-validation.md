# 0.8.0 experience-validation upgrade audit

## Goal

Improve player-experience quality without turning Godot Prototype Studio into a larger mandatory design bureaucracy. Keep the 0.7 routing/reuse/multiplayer baseline and add only evidence that closes four concrete gaps:

1. preserve the intended experience across repeated multi-system edits;
2. connect mechanic hypotheses to observable behavior rather than model taste;
3. catch gameplay rules that finish in a valid state after violating the rule during execution;
4. use real-player observation for subjective experience claims without inventing population-level certainty.

## Sources reviewed

- `saschb2b/skills` `game-design`: broad experience-design reference, especially persistent concept identity and loop-stack reasoning.
- `abagames/agentic-gamedev-skills`: mechanics as hypotheses, structural search, gameplay invariants and anti-`fun score` discipline.
- AlterLab/GameForge `game-playtest`: behavior-first playtest planning and observation.
- GameASG-Bench (arXiv:2609.21293): predeclared behavioral testing interfaces and strict core requirement acceptance.
- GameLogicBench (arXiv:2609.21562): tick-level state assertions, perturbation scenarios, composite-axis isolation and mutant-validated evaluators in Godot tasks.

Research provenance and adoption limits are recorded in `references/research-basis.md` S36-S40.

## Astra optimization decisions

The upgrade deliberately does **not** add a compulsory GDD, MDA pass, design-score rubric, fixed candidate count, mandatory multi-agent reviewer, universal tick logger, hidden test framework, or fixed human tester count.

Instead:

- `SKILL.md` remains the control plane and adds two conditional routes: experience validation and runtime logic verification.
- `experience-validation-loop.md` keeps a lightweight experience spine only for work where long-running design drift is a real risk.
- `runtime-logic-verification.md` activates only for consequential stateful rules where terminal checks can miss invalid execution.
- existing `mechanic_lab.md` gains optional behavior-contract, perturbation and evaluator-sanity fields instead of creating a second acceptance system.
- human playtests remain optional unless contracted or necessary for a subjective claim; machine-verifiable work continues without waiting for participants.

This follows the Astra principle of specifying outcome boundaries and evidence quality while leaving the model freedom to choose the shortest adequate method.

## Evidence boundaries

Machine evidence can establish implementation correctness, reachability, rule invariants, interaction effects and many degeneracies. It cannot establish delight, tension, fairness, aesthetics, social comfort or target-audience replay desire.

Human playtest evidence is separated into observed behavior, participant statement, runtime fact and interpretation. No fixed 5-person/3-of-5 threshold is adopted. Small samples are diagnostic; prevalence or market claims require a study designed for those claims.

GameASG-Bench and GameLogicBench are used as evaluation-method evidence, not as proof that this skill produces better games. Their reported agent benchmark scores are not converted into a score or quality claim for Godot Prototype Studio.

## Expected gain

The main expected gain is fewer false positives of three kinds: a locally polished feature that weakens the whole experience, a runnable game whose stateful rule is wrong in execution, and a model-authored claim that a mechanic is fun without player evidence. This is a workflow hypothesis until tested on real development tasks and player sessions.
